# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, CTOs, and technical leads evaluating senior frontend engineers. They land on the site after a referral, a LinkedIn click, or a job application. They're skimming for signal: is this person worth a conversation? Context is usually a desktop browser at a desk, daytime, professional frame of mind.

## Product Purpose

A single-page personal portfolio that functions as Àlex Castelo's professional introduction. The goal is a conversation, not a sale — the site earns a reply to a cold email or an inbound message. Success looks like: they read the whole thing, feel like they know who he is, and reach out.

## Brand Personality

Direct, confident, curious. No hedging, no self-promotion, no filler. The voice in the copy earns the voice in the design.

## Anti-references

- **Typical dev portfolio**: project grid cards, GitHub stats widgets, skill bars, hero photo with floating badges.
- **SaaS landing page**: gradient accents, feature sections with icon grids, CTA animations, dark-mode hero.
- **Recruiter-template**: resume-style layout, timeline with icons, color-coded skill tags.
- No decorative motion. No accent colors for their own sake. Nothing that reads as "made with a template."

## Design Principles

1. **The copy earns the design.** Àlex's writing is precise and unsentimental. The visual system should match that register — nothing the words didn't earn.
2. **Restraint as a signal.** What's absent communicates confidence. No decoration means the content carries the weight.
3. **Show the thinking, not the credentials.** The site is organized around how he thinks and what he values, not a list of technologies or job titles.
4. **Every element pulls its own weight.** If a component could be removed without losing meaning, remove it.
5. **Zero friction.** No JS, no animations that delay reading, no patterns that require explanation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. 4.5:1 minimum contrast for all text. Skip nav implemented. Focus indicators visible. Reduced-motion preference respected (caret blink already handled).
